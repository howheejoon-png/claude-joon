Deepwater Brand Guidelines
1. Brand Overview

Brand Name: Deepwater
Industry: AI Systems & Automation
Brand Archetype: The Strategic Operator

Brand Meaning

Deepwater represents power beneath the surface.
Like deep ocean waters where powerful forces move quietly, Deepwater builds intelligent AI automation systems that operate efficiently and invisibly beneath the surface of businesses.

Brand Personality

Intelligent

Strategic

Calm but powerful

Precise

Technical

Minimalist

Deepwater communicates strength without noise.

2. Logo System
Primary Logo

The Deepwater logo consists of two elements:

Shark emblem

Deepwater wordmark

The shark symbolizes:

precision

strength

focus

dominance in deep environments

The clean wordmark reinforces a modern AI / technology company identity.

Logo Variations
Primary Version

Red shark icon + white wordmark on dark background.

Used for:

website headers

presentations

product branding

Icon Only

The shark symbol can be used independently for:

favicon

social media avatar

app icon

product branding

Monochrome Version

For limited use:

all white

all black

Used in:

print

embossing

merchandise

3. Color System

The Deepwater palette reflects power, depth, and precision technology.

Primary Colors
Deepwater Red

Used for emphasis and iconography.

HEX

#FF2A2A

RGB

255, 42, 42

Meaning:

strength

energy

authority

Deepwater Black

Main background color.

HEX

depth

sophistication

Graphite Dark

Used for gradients and UI elements.

HEX

#1A1A1A
Neutral Colors
White

Used for typography.

HEX

#FFFFFF
Light Grey

Used for secondary UI text.

HEX

#9A9A9A
Color Ratio

Recommended usage:

70% dark tones

20% neutral greys

10% Deepwater red

The red should be used strategically as an accent, not the dominant background.

4. Typography

The typography should feel modern, minimal, and technical.

Primary Font (Headings)

Recommended fonts:

Sora

Space Grotesk

Eurostile

Orbitron

Characteristics:

geometric

futuristic

strong readability

Secondary Font (Body)

Recommended fonts:

Inter

Manrope

Roboto

These maintain clean UI readability.

5. Iconography

Icon design should follow these principles:

minimal

thin line icons

geometric

modern tech aesthetic

Icons should relate to:

AI systems

automation

network architecture

infrastructure

workflows

Avoid:

cartoon icons

overly detailed illustrations

6. Visual Style

Deepwater visuals should evoke:

deep ocean

quiet power

hidden intelligence

technical infrastructure

Common visual elements:

dark gradients

glowing highlights

data flow lines

network grids

subtle motion graphics

7. Photography / Illustration Style

Images should feel:

cinematic

minimal

high contrast

technological

Preferred imagery:

ocean depths

underwater environments

abstract technology visuals

system networks

AI infrastructure graphics

Avoid:

stock corporate photos

overly bright visuals

playful cartoon imagery

8. UI / Website Style

Deepwater’s interface should reflect a premium AI technology brand.

Design characteristics:

dark UI

subtle red highlights

minimal layout

strong typography

large negative space

Example structure:

Hero
Services
Systems
Automation Solutions
About
Contact

9. Tone of Voice

Deepwater communication should be:

clear

confident

technical

concise

Example tone:

❌ Weak tone
"We build cool AI automations."

✅ Deepwater tone
"Deepwater engineers intelligent automation systems designed to operate beneath the surface of modern businesses."

11. Brand Mission

Deepwater builds AI automation infrastructure that allows businesses to operate with greater speed, intelligence, and efficiency.

Our mission is to engineer systems that quietly power the next generation of organizations.

To become a global leader in AI-driven automation systems and intelligent infrastructure.
